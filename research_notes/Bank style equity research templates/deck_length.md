# How long single-stock pitch decks traditionally are: slide counts from real public decks

Scope note (Nov 2026): about 75 searches and fetches. Where a PDF could be fetched, pages were counted locally with macOS PDFKit (`PDFDocument.pageCount`), and slide titles were pulled from the PDF text to find section dividers and appendices. These are marked **[PDF count]**. Counts read from a hosting site's viewer (SlideShare, Scribd, Sambuz) are marked **[viewer count]**. Counts taken from news articles are marked **[article]**. Items seen only in search-result snippets, where the page itself could not be opened, are marked **[snippet, unverified]**.

Many older Sohn and contest PDFs are now dead links (404 on sohnconference.org; web.archive.org cannot be reached from this tool). Robin Hood decks are not public. So the conference sample is smaller than the 6–10 requested: 4 conference or idea-contest decks, 4 activist decks and 3 student decks.

## 1. Hedge fund conference pitches (Sohn NY/London/HK, Robin Hood, VIC, Grant's, Sohn idea contest)

### Takeaway
The public conference decks counted run **39–50 slides** (4 decks; median about 46), even though the talk slots are short: **15 minutes at Sohn NY (2015), about 12 minutes at Sohn London (2026), and 8 minutes at Sohn Hearts & Minds (2022)**. These decks are posted as "read later" documents, so they are far longer than what can be shown in the slot.

### Inferences
- Showing all 50 slides in a 15-minute Sohn slot would be about 3.3 slides a minute. Speakers clearly skip slides. The posted deck works as a leave-behind document for press and investors, not a script.
- Two kinds of "conference deck" length exist:
  - What is shown on stage: likely 10–20 slides, by the minutes available (inference).
  - What is published: about 40–50 pages, from the 4 counted decks.
- Ackman's two decks share a pattern: cover, disclaimer, a short thesis or highlights block, section dividers, a valuation range with a downside case, and a closing "summary thesis" slide. That is a good template for a single-company deck generated from a report.

### Gaps
- No counted deck from Sohn London, Robin Hood, or a 2015+ VIC. No 2017–2025 Sohn NY talk-slot length from a primary source; the 15-minute figure is from 2015.
- Only 4 conference or contest decks were counted (target was 6–10). Many Sohn PDFs on sohnconference.org are gone, and the Wayback Machine could not be reached.
- How many slides speakers actually show on stage, versus publish, is not documented anywhere I found.

## 2. Activist and short-seller presentations (Pershing, Trian, Elliott, Starboard, short decks)

### Takeaway
Activist campaign decks are a separate genre. They run from **about 35 pages** (Trian/Disney 2023 launch deck) to **168** (Pershing/ADP 2017) and **nearly 300** (Starboard/Darden 2014). They should not set the default length for a research-report deck.

### Cited Findings
- **Trian, "Restore the Magic" (Disney), 12 Jan 2023:** the DFAN14A exhibit PDF has **35 pages**, and pages 2–3 are disclaimers. The flow runs: "Why is Trian Here Today?", "Disney is Trading at an 8-Year Low", Trian overview and P&G case studies (slides 6–12), TSR and financial underperformance, M&A/Fox, succession, compensation, streaming, and cost discipline [PDF count] — [SEC EDGAR exhibit](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1744489/000090266423000183/p23-0016_exhibit1.pdf)
- **Pershing Square, ADP (2017):** Ackman spoke "near the end of a three-hour webcast and 168-slide-deck presentation" [snippet; page not opened] — [Institutional Investor](https://www.institutionalinvestor.com/article/b1505pzc7r5z53/ackman-says-changing-adp-will-likely-require-a-new-ceo)
- **Starboard, Darden (Sept 2014):** "Nearly 300 pages cover their 12 board nominees". The press called it one of the longest activist slide presentations — [WSJ MoneyBeat, 12 Sep 2014, via Shareholder Forum](https://www.shareholderforum.com/dri/Library/20140912_WSJMoneyBeat.htm)
- **Pershing Square, Herbalife (July 2014):** Ackman "flipped through a 235-page slideshow" [snippet, unverified] — [Business Insider](https://www.businessinsider.in/weve-just-witnessed-the-first-true-masterpiece-of-the-modern-hedge-fund-era/articleshow/42398824.cms)
- **Elliott, Southwest Airlines (15 Oct 2024):** the DFAN14A Exhibit 99.1 PDF is 50.6 MB, too large to fetch. Its size suggests a long deck, but the page count is not verified — [SEC filing index](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1791786/000092189524002289/0000921895-24-002289-index.htm)
- **Elliott, Phillips 66 (29 Apr 2025):** the DFAN14A exhibit is 12 pages but holds "Social Media Posts" and "Website Materials", not the "Streamline 66" deck itself. Activist DFAN14A exhibits are often fragments, not full decks — [SEC EDGAR exhibit](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1534701/000092189525001207/ex991todfan10168303_042925.pdf)
- **Short sellers (Spruce Point and others):** these publish long report-style PDFs; one Spruce Point short on FIGS was described as a 113-page report [snippet, unverified] — [Hedge Fund Alpha, Spruce Point](https://hedgefundalpha.com/strategies/spruce-point-capital-short/)

### Inferences
- Activist decks are long because they serve as proxy-fight evidence files. They cover governance, nominees, case studies of the activist's past wins and multiple disclaimers. None of that applies to a one-company research report. Their length should be quoted only as the upper bound of "pitch decks."
- Even the shortest activist deck counted (35 pages) spends about 15–20% on non-thesis material: disclaimers and the activist's own track record.

### Gaps
- The full Elliott (Southwest, Phillips 66), Starboard (2015+) and Third Point decks were not counted, either because the files are too large or because the DFAN14A exhibit held web fragments.
- No Hindenburg slide deck exists to count; Hindenburg publishes written reports, not slides. No short-seller conference deck from 2015 or later was counted.

## 3. Stock pitch competitions and student pitches (CFA Research Challenge, university competitions)

### Takeaway
Competition rules cap the **main deck at 15–20 slides, with appendix slides excluded**, and give **8–20 minutes** to present. Real CFA Research Challenge decks counted are **29–40 slides in total**, with **5–17 of those as appendix**.

### Cited Findings
- **CFA Institute Research Challenge (2026 kickoff deck):** a "10-page equity research report (up to 10 pages allowed for appendix)", and "a 10-minute presentation (additional 10 minutes for Q&A)". Judging criteria include "Materials • Quality of slides" (deck pages 18 and 36) [PDF text] — [CFA Institute 2026 Kickoff Presentation](https://higherlogicdownload.s3.amazonaws.com/CFAI/325a9907-325e-47be-8b9a-e1ceb0567276/UploadedImages/documents/RC/2026_Research_Challenge_Kickoff_Presentation_10_23_2025_FINAL.pdf)
- **Bethel University, CFA RC 2014 (Graco, HOLD, $81.50 target):** **29 slides**; slides 12–28 are appendices (financial statements, Porter's Five Forces, DCF, relative valuation, economic data, management) and slide 29 is disclosures. The main/appendix split comes from the fetch tool's reading of the viewer text, not a slide-by-slide check [viewer count] — [SlideShare](https://www.slideshare.net/slideshow/ggg-53557321/53557321)
- **UIC Blue Team, CFA RC ~2016 (Mead Johnson, HOLD, $76.02 target):** **29 slides**, of which 5 are appendix (slides 25–29: SWOT, two Porter's, two operating-income comparisons) [viewer count] — [SlideShare](https://www.slideshare.net/RiteshGhosh3/cfa-presentation-slides-final-58131567)
- **Trinity University, CFA RC local final, 5 Feb 2016 (Southwest Airlines, BUY, $59.21 target):** **40 slides**, dense with report-style text [viewer count] — [SlideShare](https://www.slideshare.net/slideshow/final-cfa-challenge-trinity-university-team-submission/71743293)
- **Martlet Fund (McGill) Flagship Pitch Competition 2025–26:** "no more than 20 slides (excluding appendices)", delivered as a PDF and PowerPoint deck plus an Excel model. Finalists get 20 minutes to present plus 10 minutes of Q&A [PDF text; pages.dev mirror] — [MFM FPC Rulebook](https://mfm-website-al2.pages.dev/pitch/MFM-FPC-Rulebook.pdf)
- **Dartmouth "Buy the DIPP":** a 15-slide cap plus a financial model, with separate-section appendix slides excluded [snippet, unverified; page returned 404 on fetch] — [DIPP competition page](https://dipp-ic.squarespace.com/competition)
- **Other time limits** [all snippet, unverified]:
  - Georgetown: up to 8 minutes, then about 10 minutes of Q&A — [Georgetown](https://finpolicy.georgetown.edu/?p=3414)
  - Appalachian State: 10 minutes — [App State](https://finance.appstate.edu/node/206)
  - UGA finals: 15 minutes plus 10 minutes of Q&A — [UGA Terry](https://www.terry.uga.edu/current-students/smif/stock-pitch-competition)
  - CFA Society Virginia: 12 minutes plus 5 minutes of Q&A — [CFA Society Virginia](https://www.cfasociety.org/virginia/society-events/stock-research-competition)
  - 2026 regional CFA stock pitch: 2 minutes in round one, 3 in the finals — [NKU](https://keyrequest.nku.edu/academics/cob/beyond/2026-stock-pitch-competition.html)

### Inferences
- The CFA Research Challenge is the closest real-world match to Liam's product: a written report (10 pages + 10 appendix) with an accompanying 10-minute deck.
- The counted RC decks hold roughly 11–24 main slides plus a large Q&A appendix. This matches the competition rules of 15–20 main slides with the appendix not counted.
- Slides per minute: a 10-minute RC talk over about 11–24 main slides is about 1–2.4 slides a minute. That is plausible for dense research slides (inference).

### Gaps
- Only 3 student decks were counted, all from 2014–2016. Newer winning RC decks appear as videos (e.g., 2026 Global Final on pulse.bot), not PDFs. University club decks found (Rochester, Delaware) sit behind Google Drive or AJAX file lists that could not be opened.
- The DIPP, Georgetown, App State, UGA, Virginia and NKU rules were not opened directly.

## 4. Sell-side and buy-side internal pitch norms (analyst pitching a PM; one-pagers; "two-slide" norms)

### Takeaway
Internal and interview pitches are **written and short, not decks**: 2–3 pages (M&I), a 2-page memo cap (Citadel's 2026 competition), or a "Tiger-style one-pager" (forum lore). No source documented a "two-slide" norm.

### Cited Findings
- **Citadel Fall 2026 Pitch Competition:** the entry is "an investment memorandum in PDF format that is no more than two (2) pages in total, including any appendix", plus an Excel valuation model, on a 6–12 month horizon. Generative AI tools may not be used to develop a submission [PDF text] — [Citadel official rules](https://www.citadel.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/Citadel-Fall-2026-Pitch-Competition-Official-Rules.pdf)
- **Mergers & Inquisitions:** an initial pitch should be "2-3 pages at the most", expanded only if you have more time, are asked to build slides, or are given a page count (from the earlier notes in this folder) — [Mergers & Inquisitions, Stock Pitch Guide](https://mergersandinquisitions.com/stock-pitch-guide/)
- **Wall Street Oasis:** a forum thread suggests "the classic Tiger 1-page format, or a bit longer, perhaps 3-5 pages" unless a presentation is requested [snippet, unverified; page returned 403] — [WSO](https://www.wallstreetoasis.com/forums/hf-analyst-pitch-examples)
- **Buy Side Insiders:** keep 20-second, 3-minute and 20-minute versions of the same pitch, with a one-page write-up and model ready [snippet, unverified; site returned 522] — [Buy Side Insiders](https://buysideinsiders.com/post/hedge-funds-101-the-20-second-20-minute-and-3-minute-stock-pitch)
- **Timed interview structure:** one guide's six parts total about 5.5 minutes (from the earlier notes) — [Wall Street Playbook](https://wallstreetplaybook.org/hedge-fund-interview-prep)

### Inferences
- The buy-side default artifact is a 1–2 page memo plus a model; a deck is a secondary format. For Tearsheet, the written report already fills the "memo" role. The deck is the presentation layer, so it can follow the competition and RC norms rather than the one-pager norm.
- A "one-slide summary" (rating, target, bear/base/bull, 3 thesis points, catalysts, risks) maps onto the one-pager tradition. It is a sensible first slide or a separate "summary" export (inference).

### Gaps
- No primary source from a sell-side firm or hedge fund states an internal page or slide limit. All internal-norm evidence here is guides, competitions and forums.
- No source for a "two-slide pitch" norm was found.

## 5. Is there an accepted length for a deck that goes with a written research report (summary vs full), and how many appendix slides?

### Takeaway
No authority sets a length for a "report-companion deck". The closest standard is the CFA Research Challenge pairing (10-page report + 10-page appendix, with a 10-minute deck). There, counted decks run **29–40 slides, with about 5–17 appendix slides**. Competition rules consistently put the main body at **15–20 slides, with the appendix excluded**.

### Cited Findings
- CFA RC: 10-page report, up to 10 appendix pages, 10-minute presentation and 10-minute Q&A — [CFA Institute 2026 Kickoff](https://higherlogicdownload.s3.amazonaws.com/CFAI/325a9907-325e-47be-8b9a-e1ceb0567276/UploadedImages/documents/RC/2026_Research_Challenge_Kickoff_Presentation_10_23_2025_FINAL.pdf)
- Appendix counts in real RC decks: 17 of 29 (Bethel 2014) and 5 of 29 (UIC 2016) — [SlideShare, Bethel](https://www.slideshare.net/slideshow/ggg-53557321/53557321); [SlideShare, UIC](https://www.slideshare.net/RiteshGhosh3/cfa-presentation-slides-final-58131567)
- Main-body caps with the appendix excluded: 20 slides (Martlet, verified), 15 slides (DIPP, unverified) — [MFM Rulebook](https://mfm-website-al2.pages.dev/pitch/MFM-FPC-Rulebook.pdf); [DIPP](https://dipp-ic.squarespace.com/competition)
- Conference decks posted alongside a talk run about 40–50 pages with no formal appendix (Ackman 2017: 50, no appendix; Doppio 2018: 44, ending on a summary-thesis slide) — [Pershing Square HHC PDF](https://assets.pershingsquareholdings.com/media/2017/05/08140311/FINAL-Ira-Sohn-Presentation-2017.05.08_Final-Sohn.pdf); [Pershing Square Doppio PDF](https://pershingsquareholdings.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/10/Doppio-1.pdf)
- Bank pitch books run 15–25 slides for initial pitches (from the earlier notes; a guide, not counted decks) — [Deckary](https://deckary.com/blog/investment-banking-pitch-book)

### Inferences
Recommended lengths for Tearsheet's "Download slide deck", synthesized from the evidence above:

| Option | Main slides | Appendix | Total | Fits |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Summary | 6–8 | 0–3 (sources, disclaimer) | ~8–10 | A 5–10 minute interview or competition talk |
| Full (default) | 12–18 | 5–10 (financial statements, model detail, peer table, sensitivity, full source list, disclosures) | ~20–28 | Matches the RC and competition norms and the 29-slide RC decks counted |
| Leave-behind | ~40–50 | — | ~40–50 | Only if the goal is a self-contained "read-later" document; not recommended as the default |

- Appendix slides should be labelled as such and kept out of the main-body count, as Martlet and DIPP do.
- Slide format: Ackman's 2017 deck is 16:9 and his 2018 deck 4:3. Either is used in practice; 16:9 is the modern default (inference).

### Gaps
- No sell-side source was found describing a standard "summary deck" issued alongside an initiation report, such as a roadshow or marketing deck. Whether banks pair initiations with a fixed-length deck is unknown.
- The sample is small (11 decks counted or reported, 7 of them from PDFs or viewers). Medians should be treated as indicative, not statistically robust.
