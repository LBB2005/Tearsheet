The chart, split beside the report

Everything you decided, in one live window:
  • From the right, as a split. The chart opens beside the report, and you drag the divider to any width. Past the text’s limit it snaps to Full, which fills everything right of the sidebar and never covers it. There is a soft snap at Half, and a double-click on the divider goes back to Half.
  • You choose what stays beside it. A Numbers / Text switch appears in the top bar while the chart is open.
  • Eight ranges: 1W, 1M, 3M, 6M, YTD, 1Y, 5Y and All. The first time you pick 5Y or All, the app downloads the company’s full history in one call. This mockup has only the 14 months already stored, so it shows those.
  • A Chart setting. Settings has a new Chart section: the chart opens at Full, at Half, or where you left it, which is the default.
  • Drag the divider between the report and the chart; watch the snap labels
  • Double-click the divider to go back to Half
  • Switch Numbers or Text in the top bar while the chart is open
  • Try 5Y or All to see the first-time download
  • Change the setting in Settings, then open the chart again
  • Hover, and drag across the chart to read a day or measure a stretch
