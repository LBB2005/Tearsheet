Look C, native: the whole app

Look C in a Mac window. There is no separate title strip: the sidebar runs the full height with the window buttons at its top, and the desktop shows through it the way it does in Finder. The glow is turned down further. Reports open on the Summary, which is built only from claims that passed the checks. The full report is one click away.

The window below is live. Click around: every page, the switch between views, the folding sidebar, the look-up box, flagging a claim and a report run all animate as they would in the app. Under it, every page and state is shown as a still.

  • Switch views: Summary or Full report, at the top of the text, or ⌘1 ⌘2
  • Look up a company: click the search box, or ⌘K
  • Fold the sidebar: the arrow at its top, or ⌃⌘S
  • Start a run: New report, then Write report; it is a sped-up simulation
  • Flag a claim: Flag, beside any claim
  • Use the menus: the menu bar along the top of the window

Shortcuts work only while Tearsheet is the app in front. Your browser keeps a few of them (like ⌘1), so here the menus and buttons do the same thing.
