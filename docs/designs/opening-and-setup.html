Opening Tearsheet: the first-time setup and the home page

Your pick (Nov 5): A. Settle for every moment. The live window uses it; the other three styles stay below for reference.

What you decided:

  • Both: a short setup the first time someone opens Tearsheet, then a home page every time after.
  • Built for someone who has never used Terminal: plain words, a copy button for anything typed, and each step ticks itself off.
  • Claude Code: two guided Terminal steps for now. A one-click install and sign-in is designed too, kept switched off until Anthropic approves it.
  • Keys: Massive is needed, because there is no rating without prices. FRED can be skipped, and the report says what’s missing.
  • The safety check runs in the background while setup carries on.
  • Home: a Claude-style “Which company today?” field. No sample report.

My calls, for you to check:

  • Setup happens inside the app window, with no sidebar, rather than in a separate setup window.
  • The home page is also New report, so ⌘N goes there.
  • The safety check shows as a small ring at the top right during setup, and in the sidebar’s status line afterwards.
  • A skipped key, or Claude Code signing out later, shows up on the home page with the fix.
  • The Massive and FRED checks prove the key works by showing one real number: SPY’s close, and the 10-year Treasury yield.

How it connects

Live

One window you can click through, as a new person. Change the motion style at any point; the window replays the current screen in that style.

  • Click Set up Tearsheet and go through each step
  • Copy on the Terminal steps: the page ticks itself off
  • Click the safety check at the top right to see what it checks
  • Paste anything as a key. A key with “bad” in it fails
  • Type a company on the home page, then press Return
  • Try the buttons below for a returning user or a sign-out
Your Mac has Reduce motion on, so the motion in this page is off.
Motion

Every moment, four ways

Ten moments from first open to a report starting, each in the four motion styles. Each plays once as it scrolls into view; Play again replays it, and Larger shows it at full width. Play all four replays a moment’s four versions side by side.

A. SettleFades and small rises, quiet and quick. The closest to macOS itself.
B. LightThings come into focus out of a blur, a soft sheen crosses each panel, and a faint light moves with you from step to step.
C. TraceLines draw themselves: panel borders, checks, rings and the links between things, like the report’s price line.
D. DepthScreens slide in from behind and step back when you move on. Checks and rings land with a small spring.

Chosen: A. Settle for every moment. This is now in the look note (docs/designs/report-look.md), and the app is built from it.
