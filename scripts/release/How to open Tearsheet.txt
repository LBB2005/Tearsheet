How to open Tearsheet
=====================

Tearsheet isn't from the App Store, and Apple hasn't checked it, so your Mac
blocks it the first time you open it. These steps get you past that. They
take about two minutes.


First, check that your Mac has Apple Silicon
--------------------------------------------

Tearsheet only works on Macs with Apple Silicon.

1. Click the Apple menu (the apple at the top left of the screen).
2. Click About This Mac.
3. Look at Chip. It should say Apple M1, Apple M2, or another Apple M chip.

If it says Intel, Tearsheet won't work on this Mac.


Open Tearsheet the first time
-----------------------------

1. Open the .dmg file you downloaded. In its window, drag Tearsheet onto the
   Applications folder.
2. Open your Applications folder and double-click Tearsheet.
3. A box says Apple couldn't check Tearsheet. Click Done.
   Don't click Move to Trash.
4. Open System Settings (Apple menu, then System Settings).
5. Click Privacy & Security.
6. Scroll down to Security. Next to the line about Tearsheet, click
   Open Anyway. Do this soon after step 3, because the button doesn't stay
   there for long.
7. Your Mac asks once more. Click Open Anyway, and type your Mac password if
   it asks for it. Tearsheet opens.


If a second box names Tearsheet Engine
--------------------------------------

Tearsheet has a helper program called Tearsheet Engine. Your Mac may block
it too, in a second box.

1. Click Done. Don't click Move to Trash.
2. Go back to System Settings, then Privacy & Security.
3. Click Open Anyway next to the line about Tearsheet Engine.
4. Quit Tearsheet (in the Tearsheet menu, click Quit Tearsheet), then open
   it again.


If your Mac says Tearsheet "is damaged"
---------------------------------------

This usually means your Mac is blocking the app, not that the download is
broken. It can happen with the zip, or when the app is opened from the
wrong place.

1. Close the box. Don't click Move to Trash.
2. Open the .dmg file and drag Tearsheet from its window onto the
   Applications folder. If your Mac asks, click Replace.
3. Open Tearsheet from your Applications folder.
4. If it still says damaged, right-click Tearsheet in Applications, click
   Open, then click Open in the box.


Each time you update
--------------------

To update, download the new .dmg and drag Tearsheet onto Applications
again. Click Replace. Your reports and settings stay.

Each new version shows these boxes again, so do these steps again each time.
Your Mac may also ask for your password so Tearsheet can use the keys it
saved before. Type your Mac password and click Always Allow.
